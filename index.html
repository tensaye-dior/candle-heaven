OUR STORY

It started in a house.

Not a factory. Not a studio. A house in Brisbane, with a pot on the stove, a kitchen bench covered in jars, and the smell of warm soy wax drifting down the hallway.

Candle Heaven began the way most honest things do: by accident, and then on purpose. We wanted a candle that smelled like the ones we'd fallen in love with in tiny shops overseas, but made here, from clean ingredients, for Australian homes. We couldn't find it. So we made it.

The first batch went to family. The second went to friends. By the third, people we'd never met were asking where they could buy one. That's when the kitchen became a workshop, and a hobby became a name.

Made in Australia. Made by hand. Made properly.

Every Candle Heaven candle is still hand-poured in Brisbane, in small batches, the same way it was on day one. We use 100% natural Australian-sourced soy wax, lead-free cotton wicks, and premium fragrance oils chosen for how they live in a room over hours, not how they perform for the first five minutes.

No paraffin. No fillers. No candle that looks better than it burns. Each one is tested for a clean, even burn of 40 hours or more, so the last light is as good as the first.

Scents that feel like home.

Australia has its own light, its own seasons, its own smell after rain. Our collection is built around that: eucalyptus mornings, coastal salt air, warm vanilla evenings, bush honey and native florals. Fragrances that belong here, made for the rooms you actually live in.

Why we do it.

We believe a room should carry a memory. The kitchen where you fed the people you love. The desk where you finally finished the thing. The quiet corner that's only yours at the end of the day.

A good candle doesn't decorate those moments. It holds them. And when it's gone, the room still remembers.

From our house to yours.

We're an Australian brand, proudly small, and growing one jar at a time. Every order is poured, labelled and packed by hand, then sent from Brisbane to homes across the country.

Thank you for lighting one of ours.
