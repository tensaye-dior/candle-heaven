CANDLE CARE

Make every burn beautiful.

Trim the wick

Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each burn.

First burn

Allow the wax pool to reach near the edge of the vessel to help reduce tunnelling.

Burn safely

Keep candles away from children, pets, drafts and flammable materials. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

Know when to stop

Stop burning when only a small amount of wax remains at the base of the vessel.
