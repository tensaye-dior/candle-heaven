Candle safety

  • Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Keep burning candles away from children, pets, drafts and anything that can catch fire.
  • Place the candle on a stable, heat-resistant surface.
  • Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each burn unless the product instructions say otherwise.
  • Do not move a candle while the wax is hot or liquid.
  • Do not burn for longer than the recommended burn period on the product label or care instructions.
  • Stop use if the vessel is cracked, damaged, excessively hot, or if only a small amount of wax remains at the base.
